    Lesson. The post-process group is named allow_seo_crawler_ua, but its action is BLOCK. It sits in the last slot, where nobody looks first. Read the action and the statement of a rule, never its name.

    Label sets are simplified for teaching. Every Bot Control label is namespaced awswaf:managed:aws:bot-control:. In a lab, bot:verified can't be reproduced: verification depends on the real crawler infrastructure. The model follows the default Bot Control version (Version_1.0): in Version_6.x, classified AI bots no longer get signal:non_browser_user_agent.
